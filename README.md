## WHO I AM

I'm an engineering student from India who never really fit into one box.

I write code and design systems, but I also care about how things *look*, how they *move*, and how they *feel* when you experience them. I'll spend an afternoon debugging a backend service and the next morning framing a photograph of light hitting a wall just right.

My brain runs on a loop of **"Okay, but can we actually make it?"** — and then I try.

I don't want to be defined by one discipline. I'm an engineer by education, but my identity doesn't live entirely inside engineering. It lives somewhere at the intersection of technology, art, curiosity, and life.

## I CREATE

Not everything I make is code.

**Photography** is probably the most personal thing I do. It's not about technically perfect shots — it's about preserving moments, places, people, small details, and moods that would otherwise disappear. I'm drawn to ordinary moments that become meaningful later.

I also care about **UI/UX**, **visual storytelling**, **art**, and **fashion**. I like things that are designed with intention — whether that's a website, a photograph, or an outfit.

I'm drawn to **interactive and dynamic experiences** — things that respond, move, and make you feel something.

## BEYOND CODE

I don't want to be reduced to my degree or my GitHub activity.

I'm a person who exists at the intersection of **technology, photography, art, gaming, fashion, sports, curiosity, and everyday experiences**. I care about how things work, but I also care about how things feel. I care about building systems, but I also care about preserving moments.

I'm curious, experimental, and sometimes chaotic in a good way. I'll start a project, pivot halfway through, and end up somewhere better than I planned. I ask "what if" more than I ask "why."

I'm building my own strange little intersection of technology, art, and life.

And I'm okay with not fitting into one box.
